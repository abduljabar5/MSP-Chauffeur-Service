A Smarter Way to the Airport

Why Riders Skip the MSP Taxi Queue

The taxi rank at MSP works the way it always has: you land, you walk to the stand, you wait for whichever cab is next, and the meter decides your fare somewhere on I-35W. A pre-booked airport taxi flips every part of that around.

I.

Flat rate instead of a meter

Your fare is fixed at booking and includes tolls and airport fees. A crash on the Crosstown or a snow-slowed 494 costs you nothing extra — the meter never runs because there isn't one.

II.

Pre-booked, pre-assigned pickup

No queue, no roulette on the vehicle's condition. You get the chauffeur's name, number, and car details by text before wheels-down, and the car that shows up is a detailed black sedan, SUV, or Sprinter.

III.

Flight tracking with a free 15-minute hold

Give us your flight number and we watch it in real time. Delays, gate changes, early arrivals — the pickup adjusts automatically, and the first 15 minutes after touchdown are always free.

IV.

Both terminals, every hour

Terminal 1 (Lindbergh) and Terminal 2 (Humphrey), arrivals and departures, 365 nights a year. A 4:30 a.m. Sun Country departure gets the same car and the same standard as a Tuesday noon Delta arrival.

On the Ground at MSP

Terminal 1 or Terminal 2 — We Know the Drill

At Terminal 1 (Lindbergh), where Delta and most legacy carriers land, your chauffeur meets you at the passenger pickup zone on the Ground Transportation level — one escalator down from baggage claim. At Terminal 2 (Humphrey), home to Sun Country and Southwest, pickup is at the ground-level curb directly outside baggage claim.

Prefer not to hunt for a curb at all? Add meet-and-greet and your chauffeur waits inside baggage claim with a name sign, takes your bags, and walks you to the car — popular for first-time visitors, grandparents, and anyone landing after a long international leg.

Heading to the airport instead? We stage pickups so you arrive on the departures ramp with time to spare — our dispatchers pad for Twin Cities rush hour and winter weather so you don't have to.

Questions, Answered

MSP Airport Taxi FAQ

How much is a taxi from MSP Airport to downtown Minneapolis? +

A metered airport taxi from MSP to downtown Minneapolis typically runs $45–$55 before tip, and more in traffic because the meter keeps running. Our flat rate is $75 for an executive sedan or $90 for an SUV — fixed before you ride, all-inclusive, with a professional chauffeur, flight tracking, and a pre-assigned car instead of a wait in the taxi line.

Where does my driver meet me at MSP — Terminal 1 or Terminal 2? +

We serve both terminals. At Terminal 1 (Lindbergh), your chauffeur meets you at the designated passenger pickup area on the Ground Transportation level; at Terminal 2 (Humphrey), pickup is at the ground-level curb outside baggage claim. You receive the driver's name, phone number, and vehicle details by text before you land, and optional meet-and-greet inside baggage claim is available.

What happens if my flight is delayed or lands early? +

Nothing you need to manage. We track your flight number in real time and adjust the pickup automatically — whether you land 40 minutes late or 20 minutes early. Every airport pickup also includes a free 15-minute hold after touchdown, so there's no waiting fee while you collect luggage.

Can I book a same-day airport taxi to MSP? +

Yes. Dispatch runs 24/7 and we regularly cover same-day and even 3 a.m. requests. For guaranteed availability we recommend booking at least 3–4 hours ahead online, or calling (612) 666-5004 for anything sooner — if a car is free, we'll send it.

Do you provide car seats or help with luggage? +

Chauffeurs load and unload all luggage at both ends of the trip — that's standard, not an extra. Forward-facing child seats and boosters are available on request at booking; just note ages in the reservation form or mention it when you call.
