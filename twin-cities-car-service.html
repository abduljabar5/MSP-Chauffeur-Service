Questions, Answered

Twin Cities Car Service FAQ

Do you only do airport runs, or can I book a ride anywhere in the Twin Cities? +

Anywhere in the metro. Airport transfers are our specialty, but roughly a third of our work is point-to-point: office to dinner in the North Loop, Edina to a Vikings game, a suburb-to-suburb business meeting, or home to a downtown gala. If both addresses are in the Twin Cities area, you can book it online with a fixed fare.

Will the driver wait during a medical appointment or dinner? +

Yes — book a round trip and tell us the expected duration. For shorter stops (under an hour) the chauffeur typically waits on site; for longer appointments we schedule a return pickup window and adjust by text if your appointment runs long. Wait time is quoted up front when you book by phone, never sprung on you afterward.

How much does car service to Rochester or the Mayo Clinic cost? +

Door-to-door sedan service from the Twin Cities to Rochester / Mayo Clinic starts around $299 fixed, with SUVs and round trips quoted individually. The quote covers everything — fuel, time, and the return leg positioning — and we run these trips daily, including early check-in appointments. Call (612) 666-5004 for an exact figure for your dates.

My suburb isn't listed — do you still cover it? +

Almost certainly. We serve the full seven-county metro — Hennepin, Ramsey, Dakota, Anoka, Washington, Scott, and Carver — plus the surrounding ring. The suburbs listed on this page are simply where we drive most often. Enter your exact addresses at book-a-ride for an instant fixed quote to or from anywhere in the metro.

How far in advance should I book a point-to-point ride? +

For metro rides, 3–4 hours' notice is usually plenty, and dispatch runs 24/7 for shorter-notice requests. For long-distance trips (Rochester, Duluth, St. Cloud), event nights, and holiday weekends, we recommend booking 24–48 hours ahead so we can guarantee your preferred vehicle.
