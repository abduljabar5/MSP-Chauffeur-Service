One Chauffeur · Your Whole Day

Private Chauffeur & Driver Service in Minneapolis

A private chauffeur is retained "as directed": the car and driver remain with you for an agreed block of time instead of ending after one drop-off. Your chauffeur waits between stops and drives wherever the day goes.

Book Your Chauffeur Call Dispatch 24/7
When It Is the Right Call

For days that do not fit in a single trip

A point-to-point ride is one trip from A to B. A private chauffeur is the right call when your day does not fit that: client visits with unpredictable timing, a full slate of meetings across the metro, a multi-stop celebration, or simply wanting a driver on call without re-booking every leg.

One driver learns your day instead of you re-explaining it at every stop. Plans change and the chauffeur absorbs it without a cancellation fee. And the hourly cost is known before the day starts.

Questions, Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between this and point-to-point? +

Point-to-point is a single trip from one address to another. A private chauffeur is retained for a block of time and stays with you across multiple stops.

Is there a minimum booking time? +

Yes, four hours. Call dispatch to confirm availability for your date.

Can my chauffeur wait while I run into a meeting? +

Yes. Waiting between stops is the whole point of as-directed service, within your booked window.

What if my day runs longer than booked? +

Additional time is billed at the same hourly rate with no multiplier. Let your chauffeur know as early as possible.

Can I request the same chauffeur for repeat bookings? +

In most cases, yes. Tell dispatch at booking and we will do our best to match you.

How do I pay? +

Card at booking or with your chauffeur. Corporate accounts can be invoiced monthly.
