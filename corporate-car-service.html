Questions, Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

How is billing handled? +

Corporate accounts are invoiced monthly, with cost-center or department coding available on request.

Can multiple people at our company book under one account? +

Yes. Any authorized employee or assistant you designate can book against the account.

Do you handle airport pickups for employees? +

Yes. Every MSP transfer under your account includes flight tracking and terminal pickup; see the MSP Airport Black Car page for details.

Can you coordinate a multi-day conference? +

Yes. Give us the schedule, headcount and locations and we will build a transportation plan, including the Sprinter for larger groups.

Is there a minimum monthly volume? +

No. Accounts are available to companies of any size.

How do we set up an account? +

Call (612) 666-5004 or use the contact page and ask for a corporate account. We will have you set up the same day.
