The Story

Founded a few minutes
from the MSP curb.

MSP Chauffeur Service started in 2012 with one black sedan and a simple observation: getting to Minneapolis–St. Paul International Airport shouldn't be the most stressful part of the trip. Meters that climb in traffic, drivers who don't know Terminal 2 exists, cars that show up late — or not at all. We decided to run it differently.

We based the operation near MSP so our cars are never far from the curb, staffed dispatch around the clock, and priced every ride as a fixed, all-inclusive fare quoted before the wheels turn. No surge. No meters. No surprises.

Fourteen years later, that one sedan has become a full house fleet — sedans, SUVs, Sprinter vans, and a stretch limousine — driven by background-checked, uniformed chauffeurs who have completed more than 50,000 airport transfers between them. The observation hasn't changed. Neither has the standard.

How We Work

Four standards.
Every ride. No exceptions.

These aren't marketing lines — they're the operating rules every chauffeur and dispatcher in the house is held to, on the first transfer and the fifty-thousandth.

I.

Your flight is watched, not asked about

Every arrival into MSP is tracked in real time. Land early or late, your chauffeur adjusts automatically — with a free 15-minute hold after touchdown, at both Terminal 1 and Terminal 2.

Fixed, all-inclusive fares set before you ride. Sedans from $60, SUVs from $75, Sprinter vans from $110. No meters running in traffic, no surge multipliers at 5 a.m.
