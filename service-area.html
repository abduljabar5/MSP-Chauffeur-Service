Coverage

Every suburb, both downtowns, and the long roads out

Our home turf is the seven-county Twin Cities metro: Minneapolis, Saint Paul and every suburb from Albertville to Woodbury. Point-to-point, hourly and airport service run across all of it at fixed fares.

Beyond the metro we regularly drive Rochester and Mayo Clinic, Duluth, St. Cloud, Mankato, Northfield, Stillwater and Hudson, and we take longer trips on request, priced as flat point-to-point fares so there is no surprise at the end. For a destination not on the list, call dispatch and we will quote it.

Questions, Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you serve the whole Twin Cities metro? +

Yes. Minneapolis, Saint Paul and every suburb across the seven-county metro.

Do you go outside the metro? +

Yes. Rochester, Duluth, St. Cloud, Mankato, western Wisconsin and beyond, priced as flat point-to-point fares.

Is there a distance limit? +

Not for point-to-point service. Hourly bookings stay within about 20 miles of the Twin Cities so the chauffeur can remain with you; longer days are quoted point-to-point.

Do you charge extra for suburbs? +

No. Fares are calculated on the routed distance, so a suburb pays the same per-mile rate as downtown.

What if my town is not listed? +

Call (612) 666-5004. If it is reachable by road, we will quote it.
