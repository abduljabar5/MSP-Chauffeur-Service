I. Executive Arrivals

Corporate Guests & VIPs

When the board member, keynote speaker, or overseas partner lands at MSP, their first impression of your company is whoever meets them. Our chauffeur is inside baggage claim with a name sign (+$15), takes the bags, and delivers them to the hotel or office rested instead of rattled. Flight tracked, discreet, invoiced cleanly to your firm.

II. Weddings

The Aisle-to-Reception Run

Ceremony to photos to reception, on a schedule that has zero slack in it — this is precision work dressed up as celebration. The stretch carries up to ten in the wedding party; pair it with a Sprinter van for the rest of the group. Packages quoted by phone, priced by the day, not the mile.

III. Prom & Milestones

Prom, Graduations, Anniversaries

For prom, parents book, set the route, and get the chauffeur's number for the night — the car stays alcohol-free and on itinerary. For milestone birthdays and anniversaries, we'll stage the stretch anywhere in the metro. Spring dates go fast; call weeks ahead.

IV. Special Events

Galas, Concerts & Nights Out

Charity galas, a suite night at the Orpheum, a tasting-menu anniversary — arrive together, leave together, and let the chauffeur solve downtown parking. For groups that don't need the stretch, our Twin Cities car service covers the same night at sedan and SUV rates.

Questions, Answered

MSP Limo Service FAQ

Can I book the stretch limousine online? +

Stretch limousine bookings are handled by phone at (612) 666-5004 so we can confirm the date, route, hours, and any decorations or beverage requests before quoting. Executive sedans and SUVs — the vehicles most airport limo bookings actually use — can be reserved online in about 60 seconds with an instant fixed fare.

How does meet-and-greet work at MSP, and what does it cost? +

For $15 on top of any transfer, your chauffeur parks, comes inside, and waits at baggage claim holding a sign with your guest's name (or your company's). They help with luggage and escort your guest directly to the car. It's the standard choice for visiting clients, international arrivals, and anyone you want welcomed properly at Terminal 1 or Terminal 2.

Do you do wedding limousine service, and how is it priced? +

Yes — ceremony-to-reception transfers, photo-stop loops, and end-of-night getaways. Weddings are quoted by phone as a package based on date, hours, and stops rather than per mile, so there are no surprises mid-celebration. The stretch seats up to 10, and many couples pair it with a Sprinter van from our fleet to move the full wedding party.

What are your prom limo policies for minors? +

A parent or guardian books the trip, receives the itinerary, and gets the chauffeur's contact for the evening. Routes are agreed in advance, the vehicle is strictly alcohol-free for minors, and our chauffeurs keep the schedule you set — dinner, grand march, venue, home. Book prom dates early; spring weekends sell out weeks ahead.

Can our company set up regular limo arrivals for visiting executives? +

Absolutely — that's a core part of our business. We can keep a profile for your firm with preferred vehicles, standing meet-and-greet instructions, and consolidated invoicing. See our MSP town car service page for corporate account details, or call (612) 666-5004 to set one up.
