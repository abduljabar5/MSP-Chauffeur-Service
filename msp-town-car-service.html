What "Town Car Service" Means Here

Discretion, Consistency, and a Driver Worth Knowing

Executive travel doesn't reward novelty; it rewards repetition done perfectly. The same clean sedan. The same ten-minutes-early arrival. A chauffeur in a jacket who knows that you take 94 to the capitol and never the surface streets, that the Thursday pickup moves to the skyway entrance, and that the call you're on is none of his business. That's the town car standard, and it's the discipline we've built since 2012 — 50,000+ transfers, 98.7% of them on time.

Our chauffeurs are employees of the standard, not gig workers of the moment: background-checked, professionally trained, and assigned with continuity in mind so account clients see familiar faces. Vehicles are late-model black sedans and SUVs — unmarked, immaculate, stocked with water and chargers — detailed before every pickup because a client's first impression of your firm may well be our back seat.

Airport work is where most engagements start: a fixed-fare run to MSP from $60, flight-tracked with a free 15-minute hold. From there, accounts typically grow into meeting circuits, dinner transfers, and full-day roadshows.

Questions, Answered

Town Car Service FAQ

How do corporate accounts work? +

We keep a profile for your firm: approved bookers, preferred vehicles, standing pickup instructions, and cost-center codes if you use them. Rides can be booked by an assistant in under a minute, executives never handle payment in the car, and accounting receives one consolidated monthly invoice with per-ride detail. Setup takes one phone call to (612) 666-5004.

Can you run a multi-stop roadshow with one car and driver all day? +

Yes — that's exactly what our as-directed service is for. One chauffeur stays with your team through every meeting: investor roadshows, site tours, client circuits between Minneapolis, St. Paul, and the 494 corridor. The car waits at each stop, luggage and materials stay secured on board, and the schedule flexes as your meetings run long or short. Quoted by phone based on hours and itinerary.

Do you offer hourly town car service? +

Hourly-style arrangements — a car and chauffeur held at your disposal — are booked by phone so we can match the right vehicle and driver to the day. Point-to-point trips with a fixed fare can always be booked online. For anything involving waiting time, multiple stops, or an open-ended schedule, call (612) 666-5004 and we'll structure it properly.

Can we request the same chauffeur every time? +

For account clients, yes, whenever scheduling allows — and we take the request seriously, because consistency is half the value of a town car service. Many of our corporate riders have worked with the same chauffeur for years: the driver knows the building's loading dock, the preferred route, and whether you want conversation or quiet.

How discreet is the service for high-profile passengers? +

Discretion is a condition of employment. Chauffeurs are background-checked professionals who sign confidentiality expectations: no commentary on passengers, itineraries, or conversations overheard in the car. Vehicles are unmarked black cars, pickups can be staged at side entrances or parking structures on request, and booking records are shared only with the account holder.
